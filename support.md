# Gymtastic Support

For help with Gymtastic, email
[gymtastic.support@gmail.com](mailto:gymtastic.support@gmail.com).

To help us investigate, include the app version, your iPhone model and iOS
version, what you were trying to do, and the steps that led to the problem.
Please do not include passwords, payment information, or personal workout or
health measurements in your message.

Gymtastic stores workout data on your iPhone. If you have enabled optional
iCloud sync, include whether sync is on and any error message you see, but do
not send exported workout files unless they are necessary to explain the issue.
