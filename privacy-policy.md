# Gymtastic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Gymtastic is an offline-first workout tracker. This policy explains what
information the app handles and the choices available to you.

## Information stored on your iPhone

Gymtastic stores the workout details, routines, intervals, custom exercises,
photos, measurements and notes you choose to save on your iPhone. The app does
not require you to create a Gymtastic account.

## Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, Gymtastic syncs saved routines, intervals, custom
exercise definitions and photos, completed workout records, and measurement
entries through your private iCloud database using your Apple Account. Sync is
off until you opt in. Active workouts and device preferences stay on your
iPhone. Turning sync off stops synchronization and preserves the local and
iCloud copies. Deletions sync when sync is enabled.

## Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional and requires your permission. Gymtastic can save
completed workout summaries and supported body measurements to Apple Health,
and read body weight, body-fat percentage and waist measurements. Imported
measurements stay on your iPhone and are not included in Gymtastic iCloud sync.
Turning off reads removes Gymtastic's imported copies, not the originals in
Apple Health. While sharing is enabled, edits or deletions of previously
exported entries are reflected in Apple Health. Manage permissions and Health
data in the Health app. Gymtastic does not use Health data for advertising or
analytics.

## Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases. Gymtastic receives verified transaction
information to determine whether Ultra features are available; it does not
receive your payment-card details. There is no Gymtastic purchase account.

## Analytics, advertising and disclosure

Gymtastic includes no advertising or analytics SDKs, does not sell your data,
and does not send workout content to developer-operated servers. Apple
processes information needed to provide iCloud, Apple Health and App Store
services under Apple's own privacy terms.

## Your choices, deletion and retention

You can edit or delete saved items in the app and export workout information as
CSV. Deletions sync when iCloud sync is enabled. Turning sync off preserves
both copies; data already in iCloud may remain there until you manage or delete
it separately. Removing the app does not necessarily remove iCloud data, device
backups or exported files. Data remains on your iPhone or in iCloud until you
delete it or manage the corresponding Apple storage.

## Privacy questions

For privacy questions, contact
[gymtastic.support@gmail.com](mailto:gymtastic.support@gmail.com). Please do not
email workout or health measurements unless they are necessary to explain your
request.
